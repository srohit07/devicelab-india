About the project

Built around a simple idea:
show your work.

DeviceLab India is an early-stage independent project intended to make consumer-device evaluations more transparent and reproducible.

Why this project exists

Product specifications do not always explain how a device behaves during sustained use. A structured evaluation can help readers understand test conditions, measured behaviour and limitations—not just a headline score.

What we plan to evaluate

The initial focus is smartphones, with possible future studies of foldables, handheld gaming PCs and consoles. Each study will be scoped to the equipment and methods actually available.

Who is behind it?

Rohit Singh — Founder and independent researcher. Rohit is building DeviceLab India as an independent, founder-led technology research project based in India. The project is currently at the setup and pilot-planning stage, with an initial focus on developing repeatable methods for evaluating smartphone performance, thermal behaviour and battery observations.

This is a new project. No completed DeviceLab India studies, laboratory accreditation, institutional affiliation or manufacturer partnerships are claimed. The first pilot will be published only after the tests have been performed and the results reviewed.

What we are not claiming

  • We are not claiming to be an accredited laboratory.
  • We are not claiming university or company affiliation.
  • We are not claiming that a study is complete until the testing and analysis are done.
  • We do not guarantee favourable conclusions to device providers.

How the project may work with manufacturers

Manufacturers may be invited to provide temporary evaluation access or, where their programmes allow, manufacturer-provided review or research units. All arrangements will be agreed in writing and disclosed. The availability of a device does not guarantee inclusion, positive coverage or a particular result.
