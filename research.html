DeviceLab India is preparing a single-device pilot. The following areas are proposed research directions, not completed studies or published results.

Current status: pilot planning.
No benchmark results or comparative rankings are claimed on this page. Research pages will be updated only after testing has been completed and the data reviewed.

Pilot 001 — Sustained smartphone performance

Primary question: How does a smartphone behave during a defined workload, and how repeatable are the measurements?

Planned observations

  • Benchmark or workload score across repeated runs.
  • Performance retention under a sustained workload, where the chosen tool supports it.
  • Battery percentage change over a fixed duration, treated as a coarse indicator rather than an energy measurement.
  • Temperature readings, with the sensor or measurement method identified.
  • Test settings, firmware, workload version, ambient conditions and deviations.

Planned output

A technical report containing the test setup, all valid runs, summary statistics, charts, limitations and a reproducibility appendix. The pilot will not rank brands because it begins with one device.

Study 002 — Foldable device usability

Potential future work may examine display workflows, multitasking, portability, software adaptation, battery behaviour and subjective usability. Hinge durability claims will not be made without a safe, properly specified test method and appropriate equipment.

Study 003 — Gaming systems

Potential future work may examine gaming performance, frame-time stability, power profiles, battery runtime, thermal behaviour and noise. Results will be compared only where test conditions are sufficiently comparable.

Study 004 — Smartwatches

Potential work may examine battery endurance, sensor consistency, connectivity, fitness-tracking behaviour and phone ecosystem integration. Sensor accuracy claims will require a suitable reference method.

Study 005 — Gaming consoles

Potential work may examine game loading behaviour, frame-rate stability where measurable, power draw, thermal behaviour and controller experience. Platform and game updates will be recorded because they can change results.

How study priorities are chosen

  1. Can the question be tested with available equipment?
  2. Can the procedure be described and repeated?
  3. Can the measurements support the proposed conclusion?
  4. Can limitations and uncertainty be reported honestly?
