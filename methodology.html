Research approach

A transparent approach
to device evaluation.

DeviceLab India is developing an evaluation programme guided by clear testing conditions, careful documentation and honest reporting. The principles below describe the approach planned for future work; they are not a claim of completed testing or laboratory accreditation.

01 — Clarity in testing

Published evaluations will identify the device tested, its software version, relevant settings and the conditions under which observations were made. Where conditions vary, those differences will be disclosed.

02 — Consistency and repeatability

Where practical, tests will be repeated to account for variation. Reports will distinguish individual observations from broader conclusions, with relevant limitations explained.

03 — Evidence over impressions

Measured results, benchmark scores and subjective user experiences will be presented as distinct forms of evidence. Conclusions will reflect what the available data can reasonably support. For example, battery-percentage change will not be presented as a direct measurement of energy use or battery capacity.

04 — Meaningful comparisons

Comparisons will account for differences in software, settings, workloads and testing conditions. Where results are not directly comparable, that limitation will be made clear.

05 — Honest limitations

Each published evaluation will acknowledge relevant equipment constraints, missing data and other factors that may affect interpretation. Planned tests will not be described as completed work.

06 — Responsible handling

Devices and data will be handled responsibly. Evaluations will respect manufacturer safety guidance, protect private information and avoid unsafe testing practices. Private identifiers and personal notifications will not be published.

07 — Independence and disclosure

Where applicable, reports will disclose whether a device was independently acquired, provided by a manufacturer for evaluation, donated or supported through another arrangement. Access to a device will not determine the conclusions, and favourable coverage will not be promised.

Current status

Evaluation programme in development

DeviceLab India is an early-stage independent research project. A full testing programme and completed evaluations are not being claimed.

Before publication

  • Describe the device and test conditions
  • Check calculations and units
  • Separate results from impressions
  • Disclose device access and support
  • State relevant limitations
