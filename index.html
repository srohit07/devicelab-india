Current project need

Seeking our first evaluation devices.

DeviceLab India is at the planning stage and does not yet have test devices. We are seeking evaluation devices from manufacturers and authorised teams so the first study can be conducted using real hardware.

An evaluation enquiry is not a claim of partnership. Any device access, restrictions, sponsorship or other support will be disclosed, and conclusions will not be promised in exchange for a device.
