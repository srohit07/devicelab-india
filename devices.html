03 / Metrics & methodology

Every result needs context.

These are the parameters we intend to record where relevant and technically measurable—not a claim that all tools or procedures are already available.

PERFORMANCE

Throughput & stability

Score or throughput, repeat-run spread, performance retention and frame-time percentiles where supported.

POWER

Energy & battery

Battery change over a defined interval; measured power only when a suitable instrument is available.

THERMALS

Temperature

Sensor or instrument source, ambient conditions, workload duration and measurement limitations.

NETWORK

Latency & reliability

Median and tail latency, test endpoint, connection type, repeat count and packet loss when measured.

PROTOCOL

Reproducibility

Device model, firmware, app versions, settings, run order, duration and deviations.

DISCLOSURE

Device provenance

Loan or ownership status, support restrictions, sample limitations and conflicts of interest.

i
Standards and tools will be named only when actually used.

We will not claim ISO certification, laboratory accreditation or compliance with a standard unless the applicable scope and evidence support that claim. Test procedures will cite relevant standards or tool documentation when appropriate.
